Yulia Valdivia Rivera

CV · email · Harris · LinkedIn

I am a Ph.D. student at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. I work on labor economics, public economics, and inequality, with a focus on migration, trade, and the enforcement of labor market policy.

Before Harris I was a research assistant in the World Bank’s Poverty and Equity Global Practice and an economist at the Peruvian Institute of Economics. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Economics from Universidad del Pacífico.
