Yulia Valdivia Rivera

Ph.D. student in Public Policy, Harris School of Public Policy, University of Chicago

I am a labor economist working on immigration. I study how the rules that govern access to work, such as work authorization, shape employment outcomes for immigrants and the native-born workers they work alongside, and how changes in who migrates reshape local labor markets.

Before Harris, I was a research assistant in the World Bank’s Poverty and Equity Global Practice and an economist at the Peruvian Institute of Economics. I hold a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. in Economics from Universidad del Pacífico.
