Hello. I am a Ph.D. student at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. I work mainly on labor economics, public economics, and inequality.

CV yvaldiviarivera@uchicago.edu

My research looks at how migration policy, trade agreements, and the enforcement of labor regulations shape economic opportunity for workers and households. You can find current papers on the research page.

Before coming to Chicago I was a research assistant in the World Bank’s Poverty and Equity Global Practice, where I worked on Peru’s Poverty Assessment, fiscal incidence analysis, and the Country Climate and Development Report. I previously worked as an economist at the Peruvian Institute of Economics. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Economics from Universidad del Pacífico.

If you would like to get in touch, email is best. I am also on LinkedIn and in the Harris directory.
