About

I am a Ph.D. student at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. My current research is in labor economics, with a focus on immigration and local labor markets in the United States. I study how lawful migration pathways and changes in the origins of immigrant inflows affect workers and communities.

Before Harris I was a research assistant in the World Bank’s Poverty and Equity Global Practice and an economist at the Peruvian Institute of Economics. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Economics from Universidad del Pacífico.
